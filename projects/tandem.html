Work / FBR-26-03 / AI automation studio

Tandem

“AI that works alongside you.”

ClientTandem
SectorAI automation studio
Year2026
ServicesBrand identity, Website, Positioning, Messaging
Liveheytandem.co.uk ↗
01Context

Tandem needed a brand and website that could make AI feel practical, useful and easy to understand. In a market full of vague AI promises and overcomplicated consultancy language, the goal was to create something sharper — a brand that clearly shows how AI agents and automations work alongside real teams to remove repetitive manual work.

We shaped the whole experience around clarity, confidence and outcomes. Rather than presenting Tandem as another futuristic AI company, the brand was built to feel grounded, operator-led and commercially useful.

02The challenge

AI is a crowded space, and most businesses are still unsure what it actually means for them. Tandem had to explain a technical service without feeling intimidating, vague or consultancy-led — and land the offer within seconds.

  • 01Make AI agents and automation feel practical, not abstract
  • 02Build a clear offer around time saved and operational efficiency
  • 03Strip out AI buzzwords and empty future-talk
  • 04Win trust with businesses that are curious but cautious
  • 05Position Tandem as operators who build working systems, not slide decks
  • 06Move visitors from problem to solution fast
03The approach

We focused the brand around one core idea: AI should pull its weight. The site shows that Tandem doesn’t advise on AI — it builds systems that work inside the tools and processes businesses already use.

The design direction is sharp, dark and system-led, with messaging that keeps the offer direct and commercially grounded. The site is structured around the services that matter — AI agents, workflow automation, custom systems and training — with clear examples of the manual work each one removes.

04Delivered
Brand direction Website design + build Service positioning Messaging system Conversion-led CTAs Process, proof, pricing + FAQ architecture
05Outcome

Tandem launched with a brand that makes a technical offer feel simple: modern and intelligent, but direct enough for a busy owner to understand in one scroll.
