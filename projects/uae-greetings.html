Work / FBR-26-08 / E-commerce

UAE Greetings

“Heartfelt cards, sold beautifully.”

ClientUAE Greetings
SectorE-commerce
Year2026
ServicesE-commerce design, Brand presentation, Store build
StatusShipped
01Context

UAE Greetings sells cards made to be kept — and the store had to treat them that way. The brief was an e-commerce experience where warm, personal products are presented beautifully without slowing down the path to purchase.

This is a shorter case file: the work speaks in the storefront itself.

02The challenge

Greeting cards are an emotional purchase made quickly. The store needed personality at first glance and zero friction from product to checkout.

  • 01Product-first presentation that feels warm, not clinical
  • 02A browsing experience built for gifting occasions
  • 03A short, simple path from product to payment
  • 04A brand presence that scales with the range
03The approach

We designed the store around the cards themselves — generous product imagery, clear occasion-led navigation and a checkout flow with nothing in the way.

04Delivered
Store design Product presentation system Occasion-led navigation Checkout-first UX
05Outcome

A storefront that sells the feeling as well as the card — and stays out of the way when it’s time to buy.
