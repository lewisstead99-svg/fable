Work / FBR-26-02 / Estate agency

Key Drummond

“Prime coastal property, presented properly.”

ClientKey Drummond
SectorEstate agency
Year2025–26
ServicesProperty marketing, Campaign design, Social content system, Print
StatusShipped
01Context

Key Drummond already had an established name across the Poole, Lilliput, Canford Cliffs and Sandbanks property market. The job wasn’t to reinvent the brand — it was to support it with stronger visual marketing across social media, property campaigns and day-to-day promotional content.

We created clean, property-led design assets that help Key Drummond communicate listings, market updates, open days and brand messages in a more polished, consistent way — matched to the expectations of a high-value coastal market.

02The challenge

In estate agency, presentation sells — especially when the stock is premium coastal homes. The marketing needed to feel elevated, clear and recognisable, while staying flexible enough for daily social use.

  • 01Stronger visual consistency across social content
  • 02Property posts that feel premium and attention-grabbing
  • 03Clear assets for open days, new listings and campaigns
  • 04Professional without feeling cold or corporate
  • 05Sharper presentation of property details, photography and CTAs
  • 06Showing up online with more polish and confidence
03The approach

One simple rule: the property is always the hero. The design system supports the photography rather than overpowering it — clean layouts, refined typography, strong spacing and subtle branded details.

Social content is built to work in-feed within seconds: clear hierarchy, strong imagery, and messaging that makes the value of every post obvious. From listing graphics to campaign assets, everything is consistent, professional and easy for the agency to use.

04Delivered
Social media design system Property marketing graphics New listing + open day assets Campaign visuals Story + feed layouts Enquiry and viewing CTAs Print — Spring Edition brochures
05Outcome

A marketing system that keeps every touchpoint — from a story frame to a printed brochure — at the standard the properties deserve.
