Work / FBR-25-05 / Luxury stays

Drift Collective

“Coastal living, curated properly.”

ClientDrift Collective
SectorLuxury stays
Year2025
ServicesBrand identity, Website, Positioning, Print
StatusShipped
01Context

Drift needed a brand and digital presence that reflected the quality of the homes it represents. Operating in the high-end short-term letting market, the brand had to work twice: building trust with property owners while creating desire for guests looking for a more elevated stay.

The goal was to move away from the feel of a standard holiday-let company — towards something refined, calm and lifestyle-led, built around premium coastal living and thoughtful hospitality.

02The challenge

Luxury that feels forced isn’t luxury. In a market full of generic holiday-let brands, Drift needed an identity that felt boutique, trustworthy and aligned with the calibre of homes it wanted to represent.

  • 01A premium identity for luxury short-term stays
  • 02A calmer, more lifestyle-led web experience
  • 03Trust with high-value property owners
  • 04Homes presented with desire and professionalism
  • 05Hospitality warmth balanced with management credibility
  • 06Messaging that works for guests and owners alike
03The approach

We treated Drift as more than a booking platform — a careful, hands-on hospitality partner that understands presentation, guest experience and the expectations that come with high-end homes.

The visual direction is refined and spacious: clean layouts, soft coastal cues and premium property imagery that create a sense of calm. Messaging focuses on ease, care and attention to detail.

04Delivered
Brand identity Website design + structure Owner-side positioning Guest-facing copy Property-led layouts Print + stationery Campaign direction
05Outcome

A hospitality brand that earns both audiences: owners see a credible custodian for their property, guests see a stay worth wanting.
