Work / FBR-26-07 / Estate agency

Davidson Estates

“Sales & lettings, properly presented.”

ClientDavidson Estates
SectorEstate agency
Year2026
ServicesBrand refinement, Website, UX structure, CTA strategy
StatusShipped
01Context

Davidson Estates needed a website and brand experience aligned with the level of service they actually provide. The foundations of a trustworthy agency were there — but the digital presence lacked the polish, structure and confidence to stand out in a competitive local market.

We refined the visual direction, improved the website experience and created a clearer, more professional way for customers to understand the brand — established, approachable and premium, without losing the personal feel of an independent agency.

02The challenge

The agency had the local knowledge. The brand needed more authority — and the online experience had to build trust faster with buyers, sellers, landlords and tenants.

  • 01Consistency across every digital touchpoint
  • 02Trust, service and market expertise communicated instantly
  • 03Cleaner structure for property pages and services
  • 04Premium without becoming cold or corporate
  • 05Clearer CTAs for valuations, listings and enquiries
03The approach

We simplified the direction: clarity, strong typography, clean layouts and a warmer visual system that works across both sales and lettings.

The website feels modern but familiar — giving users what they need quickly while keeping the experience calm and professional. Every section is structured around trust: who Davidson Estates are, what they offer, where they operate, and how easy the next step is.

04Delivered
Refined visual direction Website design system Services + listings structure Valuation-led CTAs A digital presence built for long-term growth
05Outcome

An independent agency that now reads as established and premium on first contact — with clearer journeys to the enquiries that matter.
