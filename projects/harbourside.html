Work / FBR-25-11 / Property services

Harbourside Inventories

“The detail people, given the details.”

ClientHarbourside Inventories
SectorProperty services
Year2025
ServicesBrand direction, Marketing design, Messaging, Stationery
StatusShipped
01Context

Harbourside Inventories needed a more polished way to communicate its services to landlords, letting agents and property professionals. In a sector where accuracy, speed and reliability decide who gets hired, the brand had to feel credible from the first interaction.

The aim wasn’t to overcomplicate a practical service — it was to make it sharper, more consistent and better suited to the property market it serves.

02The challenge

Inventory services live on trust. Agents and landlords comparing providers need to feel the reports will be accurate, thorough and professionally delivered — communicated in seconds, not paragraphs.

  • 01A more professional, trustworthy presence
  • 02Services communicated clearly and simply
  • 03A cleaner visual style for property-focused marketing
  • 04Confidence with landlords, agents and property managers
  • 05Organised, accurate and dependable — visibly
  • 06Assets ready for outreach and enquiries
03The approach

Clarity over noise. A clean, dependable visual presence that makes the service easy to understand and simple to enquire about.

Structured layouts, clear typography and property-led messaging — every element designed to support confidence, from how services are explained to how the brand appears across digital, print and marketing.

04Delivered
Brand + visual direction Marketing design support Service messaging Property-led layouts CTA structure Stationery system
05Outcome

A detail-obsessed service that finally looks like one — organised, accurate and easy to choose.
