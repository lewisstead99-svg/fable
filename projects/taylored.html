Work / FBR-26-01 / Grooming

Taylored

“The new standard for cuts, shaves and culture.”

ClientTaylored Barbering
SectorGrooming
Year2026
ServicesBrand identity, Website, Shopfront, Print
StatusShipped
01Context

Taylored Barbering needed an online presence that matched the quality of the service delivered in the chair. The brand already had a strong local feel, but the digital experience needed more polish, clearer messaging and a stronger visual direction to reflect the standard of the cuts, the atmosphere of the shop and the loyalty of its client base.

The goal: premium without being overdone, stylish without being intimidating, and simple enough to turn visitors into bookings.

02The challenge

For a service-led brand, first impressions decide bookings. The foundations were strong — the presentation needed to work harder.

  • 01A more consistent, professional visual style
  • 02A website that communicates quality in seconds
  • 03A booking journey that feels simple and direct
  • 04Premium grooming balanced with local approachability
  • 05Clearer, more actionable service information
  • 06A polished experience across mobile and desktop
03The approach

We built the direction around craft. Clean, considered and masculine, with enough personality to stand out from generic barbershop sites — bold typography, strong spacing, refined imagery and a simple layout system.

The site is structured around the actions that matter: understand the shop, see the standard, check services, book. Every section kept clear and intentional.

04Delivered
Visual identity refinement Website design system Booking-led CTAs Mobile-first experience Shopfront + print Copy focused on craft and confidence
05Outcome

A brand experience that feels precise and confident — established enough for new clients to trust, simple enough for regulars to book in seconds.
