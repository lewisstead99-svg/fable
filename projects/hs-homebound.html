Work / FBR-25-06 / Property

H+S Homebound

“Thoughtfully crafted, bespoke homes.”

ClientH+S Homebound
SectorProperty
Year2025
ServicesCreative direction, Brand system, Website, Site hoarding
StatusShipped
01Context

H+S Homebound needed a visual and digital presence that could communicate more than a service. The brand had a strong idea behind it — rooted in home, lifestyle and emotional connection — but needed a clearer system to bring that idea to life across the website, content and customer journey.

We defined the creative direction, sharpened the presentation and built a more elevated online experience: personal, premium and easy to understand, with the structure to grow.

02The challenge

The emotional foundation was strong. The brand needed a visual language that felt soft and human without becoming vague — and premium without feeling cold.

  • 01The brand idea communicated clearly at first glance
  • 02Consistency across web and social
  • 03A calmer, more intentional website structure
  • 04Lifestyle warmth balanced with commercial credibility
  • 05A more polished, memorable, trustworthy experience
03The approach

We grounded the brand in a simple idea: home should feel considered, personal and beautifully presented. From there — soft editorial layouts, warm imagery, refined typography and a more spacious web experience.

The point was never to over-design it. Every section gives the brand room to breathe, guiding people through the story while keeping the experience clean and calm.

04Delivered
Creative direction Brand design system Website layouts Messaging + purpose story Site hoarding — The Treehouse Social + campaign styling
05Outcome

An identity that carries feeling without losing structure — warm where it matters, commercial where it counts.
