Work / FBR-26-04 / Proptech

ReShot

“You snap it. We fix it.”

ClientReShot
SectorProptech
Year2026
ServicesBrand identity, Product design, Website, Mascot system
StatusShipped
01Context

ReShot was created to solve a simple but common problem in estate agency: property photos often need to look better, but agents don’t always have the time, budget or workflow for a traditional editing process.

We built the identity, website and product experience around one clear promise — upload a photo, enhance it, get a polished result fast. A direct, memorable platform for agents who want better listings without more work.

02The challenge

Estate agents are busy, impatient and surrounded by software that overcomplicates simple tasks. ReShot had to communicate its value immediately.

  • 01An offer understood in seconds
  • 02Modern without becoming too technical
  • 03Before-and-after results as the main proof
  • 04A site that drives sign-ups and first uploads quickly
  • 05Simple enough for everyday agency use
  • 06Pricing and turnaround that feel clear and low-risk
03The approach

Everything is built around clarity. A bold, clean, instantly recognisable identity — a sharp blue-led system, direct messaging and a simple product flow that’s easy to act on.

Rather than over-explaining the technology, the results are the hero: before-and-after visuals, short copy and clear calls to action show agents exactly what ReShot does and why it matters.

04Delivered
Brand identity + mascot Conversion-focused website Product UX — upload-led journey Before/after proof sections Pricing + CTA structure Messaging built on speed and ease
05Outcome

A proptech brand that gets out of its own way — the product promise lands on first contact, and the path from visitor to first upload is friction-free.
